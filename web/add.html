Each device, about a minute

  1. Plug it into this Mac with a USB-C data cable (Chrome or Edge).
  2. Press Install and pick your Wi-Fi when it asks.
  3. Close the window. It pairs itself and appears on the control page. Hang it up.

Lights need no cable: paste the Govee key, tick the bulbs, say where each one is. Devices update their own firmware after that.

No new screen

Your own TV + a Raspberry Pi on a spare HDMI

Atlántico becomes what the TV rests on. Any button on the remote goes straight back to TV and apps; when you finish watching it comes back by itself; it wakes for arrivals only if the TV is off (never over a film) and lets the TV sleep when nobody is staying. Whoever arrived or left meanwhile is shown when it returns.

Lamps that follow the picture (optional): Govee lights with LAN Control (TV light bars, floor or table lamps; ordinary Govee bulbs can't change fast enough) placed left, right or behind the TV. On Atlántico they follow the wall and its surprises; while you watch they follow the film, side by side, from a Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 facing the screen.

Just Atlántico, on a big screen

A matte art TV + a Raspberry Pi behind it

The TV is the canvas: Hisense CanvasTV 55″ (best value), Samsung The Frame or TCL NXTFRAME, all matte so they read like a print. A Raspberry Pi 5 (about $90 with case and power) plugs into its HDMI and only ever shows the wall: on at power-up, back by itself if anything closes, and it switches the TV off at night and on in the morning.

Small and simple

A matte paper-like tablet

TCL NXTPAPER 14 (14.3″, about 760 g) or NXTPAPER 11 (about 500 g). The matte screen doesn't reflect, so by day the light palettes read like printed paper; at night the dark sky glows softly. Full colour and motion, around $170–350.

Holds it: a slim case with 4 pairs of Command Large picture-hanging strips (rated 16 lb ≈ 7 kg for four pairs, so about ten times the weight), or a tabletop easel stand on a shelf or sideboard. Strips need a smooth painted wall: not gotelé (the textured plaster common in Spanish flats), wallpaper or paint less than a week old. On those, use the stand.

The big statement

A matte art TV

Hisense CanvasTV (best value, free art library), Samsung The Frame (most polished) or TCL NXTFRAME (frame in the box). Matte, like a framed print, 55″ and up. Atlántico runs in the TV's browser, not its Art Mode.

Holds it: a 55″ CanvasTV is about 18 kg, far too heavy for any adhesive. Use a floor easel stand rated 35 kg or more for 43–65″ (VIVO, ECOTINY or KONIC tripods, about $100), or Samsung's Studio Stand for The Frame. Keep it against a wall, away from where people walk; skip any anti-tip strap that needs a screw.

Deepest night sky

An OLED screen

True black: at night only the lines and stars glow. Any OLED iPad, tablet or TV. Turn on OLED care below; it moves the picture a few pixels now and then so the captions can't burn in.

Already have one

Any iPad, tablet, TV or old laptop

It works today. A glossy screen shows reflections; a matte screen protector (Paperlike or similar) fixes most of that on a tablet.

On the screen, open the wall

Scan the code with the tablet's camera, or type the address into the TV's browser. A small card with a 4-digit code appears in its corner.

Make it stay on, full screen
  1. iPad: Share › Add to Home Screen, open it from there, then Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access (triple-click to start). Settings › Display › Auto-Lock › Never.
  2. Android tablet (NXTPAPER): install Fully Kiosk Browser, set the Start URL to the address above, and turn on "Keep screen on" and "Launch on boot". Or use Chrome's Add to Home Screen.
  3. Smart TV: open the address in its web browser. Turn off Auto Power Off, Sleep timer and screen saver in the TV's settings.
  4. Fire TV or Chromecast stick: install a browser (Silk, TV Bro or Fully Kiosk) and open the address.
