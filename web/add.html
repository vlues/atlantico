Each device, about a minute

  1. Plug it into this Mac with a USB-C data cable (Chrome or Edge).
  2. Press Install and pick your Wi-Fi when it asks.
  3. Close the window. It pairs itself and appears on the control page. Hang it up.

Lights need no cable: paste the Govee key, tick the bulbs, say where each one is. Devices update their own firmware after that.

What to get

Prices are rough (October 2026); check before buying. Everything runs on USB-C 5 V and needs no drilling. Links search Amazon.es, Amazon.com and the maker's shop.
